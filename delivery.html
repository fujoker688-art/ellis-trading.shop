Shipping & Delivery

Delivery Area

We currently deliver throughout Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, and the New Territories.

Shipping Costs

  • Standard Delivery: HK$80 — Free for orders over HK$1,000
  • Express Delivery: HK$150 — Next-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM

Delivery Times

  • Standard: 2–4 business days
  • Express: Next business day (order before 2 PM)

Wine Shipping

All wine orders are packed with protective packaging to ensure safe transit. We recommend having someone available to receive the delivery, as wine shipments require a signature.

Returns & Damages

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours with photos of the damage and we will arrange a replacement or refund. Due to the nature of wine and beauty products, we cannot accept returns of opened items unless they are faulty.

For any questions, please contact us.
